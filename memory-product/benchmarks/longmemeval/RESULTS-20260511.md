## What Didn't Work

### Fact Extraction (Root Cause of 0.0% Accuracy)

**Problem**: System extracts full conversation turns as "Raw turn" memories instead of identifying atomic facts.

**Example**:
- **Question**: "What degree did I graduate with?"
- **Expected fact**: "User graduated with Business Administration degree"
- **Actual memories**:
  - "Raw turn — 2026-05-11 01:23:40 UTC" (importance: 0.3)
  - "Raw turn — 2026-05-11 01:24:29 UTC" (importance: 0.3)
  - Long conversation about Hawaii vacation planning
  - Podcast recommendations
  - Data science certification discussions

**Outcome**: Recall retrieves WRONG memories (unrelated conversations). The "Business Administration" fact exists somewhere in the 125 stored memories but is buried in verbatim conversation dumps.

### Recall Precision

**Symptom**: Recall returns 0 chars of relevant context for all 5 questions.

**Investigation**: Recall IS retrieving memories, but they're semantically mismatched:
- Query: "What degree did I graduate with?"
- Retrieved: Vacation planning, entrepreneurship podcasts, policy debates
- NOT retrieved: The specific "Business Administration" fact

**Root cause**: Without atomic fact extraction, the similarity search can't distinguish between:
- "User discussed college" (general topic)
- "User graduated with Business Administration degree" (specific fact)

### CP11+ Priorities (Fact Extraction Optimization)

#### 1. Atomic Fact Identification (Highest Priority)
**Goal**: Extract discrete facts instead of "Raw turn" dumps.

**Approach**:
- LLM prompt engineering: "Extract atomic facts from this conversation turn"
- Example input: "I graduated with a degree in Business Administration from UCLA in 2018."
- Example output facts:
  - "User graduated with Business Administration degree" (importance: 0.9)
  - "User attended UCLA" (importance: 0.8)
  - "User graduated in 2018" (importance: 0.7)

**Success metric**: >80% of memories have importance >0.7 (vs. current 0.3 for "Raw turn")

#### 2. Memory Type Tagging
**Goal**: Classify facts by type for better recall ranking.

**Types**:
- `education` (degrees, schools, certifications)
- `preference` (likes, dislikes, opinions)
- `event` (timestamped actions, activities)
- `relationship` (people, connections)
- `goal` (aspirations, plans)

**Recall boost**: Prioritize fact-type memories over raw-turn memories.

#### 3. Importance Calibration
**Goal**: Assign higher importance to standalone facts vs. conversational filler.

**Heuristics**:
- Atomic facts: 0.7-0.9
- User preferences: 0.6-0.8
- Conversational acknowledgments: 0.1-0.3
- "Raw turn" fallback: 0.3 (current default)

#### 4. Re-run Benchmark (After Optimization)
**Target**: >50% accuracy (vs. 0.0% baseline)
**Competitive gap**: Close from 93.4pp → 43pp (vs. Mem0)
